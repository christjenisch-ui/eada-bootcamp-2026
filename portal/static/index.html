    How this works

    The model learned from 2,930 home sales in Ames, Iowa between 2006 and 2010 (the De Cock "Ames Housing" dataset). Ownly asks eight questions; everything else the model needs (lot, basement, roof and so on) is filled in from a representative home of the same type in the chosen neighbourhood.

    Not covered: apartments, homes built after 2010, and homes outside Ames. Neighbourhood markers are approximate centres, not boundaries.
