Takeaways

    KPI glossary

    RMSE
    Root mean squared error, in $1000s. The typical size of a prediction miss, with big misses penalised extra.
    CV RMSE
    RMSE averaged over 5 cross-validation folds of the training data. More stable than a single test split, so it is used for ranking.
    MAE
    Mean absolute error, in $1000s. The average miss, without extra weight on large errors.
    R²
    Share of price variation the model explains. 1.0 is perfect, 0 is no better than guessing the average.
    MAPE
    Mean absolute percentage error. The average miss as a % of the true price.
    Overfit gap
    Train R² minus test R². Large values mean the model fits training data much better than new data.
    Fit time
    Seconds to train once on the training houses.
